[h2] Emergency Protocols & OISD Guidelines
[p] Automated mitigation strategies, operator actions, and OISD safety standard compliance protocols.
[p] Safety notes
[h3] What to do for this scenario
[p] Plain guidance based on the scenario you selected. Use with your site's official procedures.
[p] The risk
[p] Liquid trapped between valves
[p] Liquid stuck in a pipe can heat up and burst the pipe. When it breaks, fuel vapor escapes and can explode.
[p] Equipment fixes
[li] Install relief valves on pipe sections that can be closed off.
[li] Send relief lines to a safe flare or low-pressure header — not open air.
[li] Use valves that release pressure if trapped liquid expands.
[p] Operator actions
[li] Drain pipes before locking valves closed (LOTO).
[li] Check line temperatures daily, especially on sun-exposed pipes.
[li] Limit transfers in very hot weather for light hydrocarbons.
