# Brag Plan: Threat Zone

## What is this app?
Threat Zone is a physics-based industrial consequence visualizer for Indian oil & gas refineries and petrochemical plants, modeling catastrophic Vapor Cloud Explosions (VCE) and BLEVE fireballs with real-time blast rings, thermal radiation, and evacuation zoning on live satellite maps.

## The angle
High-stakes industrial disaster simulation meets precision refinery command room engineering. No generic SaaS fluff: pure, raw physics modeling real Indian facilities (Jamnagar, Panipat, Kochi) with overpressure decay, TNT equivalencies, and emergency hazard radii calculated in 420 milliseconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
High-altitude satellite targeting crosshairs snap onto the world's largest refinery complex (Jamnagar, Gujarat) under ominous dark-room lighting with bold warning typography: "WHEN 50 TONNES OF HYDROCARBON FLASH..."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Live Physics Dial-In**: BLEVE detonation toggle activates, dialing up tank pressure to 18.5 bar as TNT equivalence metrics and fireball dimensions surge.
- **Concentric Detonation Mapping**: Real-world satellite view illuminates with 4 concentric blast zones (Zone 1 Severe `#EF4444` demolition ring expanding outward to Zone 4 Safe Perimeter `#0284C7`) with live wind vector dispersion.
- **Emergency Action Telemetry**: Real-time evacuation threshold advisory (2,450 m perimeter) and structural decay metrics lock into place.

## Outro / punchline
"MODEL THE WORST CASE. BEFORE IT HAPPENS." followed by the authoritative Threat Zone industrial brand mark and consequence modeling badge.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Target facility selection (Jamnagar Refinery Complex, 1.4 MMbpd) with ambient meteorological conditions.
2. **Key Action**: Toggle to BLEVE explosion mode and adjust storage containment pressure (18.5 bar, Propane C₃H₈).
3. **Result**: Physics engine detonates 4-tier overpressure blast zones, computing exact evacuation boundary (2,450 m) and live damage consequence advisories.
